### PART 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Nightstone was attacked by cloud giants, who bombed the settlement with rocks and stole the village’s defensive landmark (the obsidian nightstone). Most villagers fled, but many were captured by goblins that moved in to plunder the ruins.

The adventuring party arrived post-attack and systematically cleared the village of goblins. During this process, they stabilized critical situations: they rescued surviving guards and a trapped Zhentarim spy (Kella) in the Inn.

Simultaneously, two rival mercenary groups arrived: rival orcs from the hills and the Seven Snakes Zhentarim, led by Xolkin Alassandar. Through a combination of diplomacy, strength, and the common threat of the orc raid, the party forged an unlikely, temporary alliance between the Zhents, the surviving Nightstone militia, and wood elf scouts who had arrived from the Ardeep Forest.

In the final preparation phase, the party executed a dramatic cavalry blitz, killing the final worg sentries that had been blockading the main square, thereby freeing the main drawbridge for the entire coalition. A critical ten-minute preparation window (Operation: Lawful Requisition) was spent gathering specific gear, allocating supplies, and casting protective magic before riding out.
