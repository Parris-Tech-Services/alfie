🌾 Goldenfields — Location Guide
The Granary of the North · Walled temple-farm of Chauntea · ~5,000 residents

Overview: Huge walled temple-farm run by Abbot Ellardin Darovik (Emerald Enclave). 60-ft outer wall, 20-ft inner side. South gatehouse entrance. Scouts at watch posts. Scale wall DC 15 Athletics (eroded mortar provides handholds). More than 20 square miles of tillage.

Key Locations

G1 — Gatehouse (South wall)

Three shifts. Each has priest, 4 acolytes, hired mage, 10 scouts, 30 guards. Gates: AC 16, 500 HP, damage threshold 10. Castellan Hantanus Tarm (boorish, no military skill). All wagons searched before entry.

G2 — Watch Posts (perimeter)

30 scouts per post, 20 on duty, 10 resting. 12 on parapet facing out, 8 patrol walls in pairs. Alarm: strike pagoda gong → heard 2 miles, cascades to other posts. Too far to hear Oren's shout from the town.

G6 — Town (concentric longhouses around central square)

Mostly human commoners. Grove of trees south of town has 3 young treants (awaken to defend abbey). Lifferlas (awakened tree) lives here — old, knows stories, loves children.

G7 — Harvesthome Abbey

Largest building. Black bears Darlow and Tilbee wander inside. Abbot leads prayers 9am, noon, 3pm, 6pm. Acolyte Zi Liang patrols outer walls when off-duty. No guards inside, just acolytes.

⚠ Hill Giants Lob & Ogg eventually head here during the attack.

G8 — Northfurrow's End (large inn, north of town)

Run by Miros Xelbrin (party's quest contact). 3-storey stone inn. Free room & board for Emerald Enclave members. Naxene Drathkala (mage) in suite above stable house. Oren Yogilvy (halfling bard) lives here. Goldengulp beer.

⚠ Party stays here. Attack begins when Oren shouts the alarm from outside.

G9 — Goldengulp Brewery + Dragon's Spire tower

Captain of Guard Strog Thunderblade HQ. Four stories, flat roof with green marble battlements. Strog is fat, lazy, sleeps late. Guards at base and top. Brewery locked at night (DC 15 Dex to pick).

⚠ Too far to hear Oren's alarm. Guards there do NOT respond initially.

G13 — Wheat Fields (eastern side)

Goblins and bugbears enter here at point X on map, undetected (Strog failed to brief guards). 10-ft tall stalks in season. Hill Howlers gang gets LOST here and emerge near G4 grain towers by mistake.

🏹 Attack on Goldenfields
Three monster gangs — triggered by Oren's shout at night
⚔ Setup: Characters are at Northfurrow's End (G8). Oren shouts the alarm. Low mist in gardens. Three monster gangs already inside the wall.

Monster Gangs Inside the Wall

🗡 Eye Stabbers

2 ogres + 4 bugbears + 10 goblins → heading for town (G6) — Oren spots these first

🌾 Hill Howlers

2 ogres + 4 bugbears + 10 goblins → lost in wheat fields (G13), emerge near G4 grain towers, then head for abbey (G7)

🏔 Giants Lob & Ogg

Outside wall with sacks, waiting for food thrown over. After bell rings: climb in instead of fleeing. Head for abbey without greatclubs. Surrender at ≤15hp.

Outside the Wall (still a threat)

4 more hill giants + 6 ogres with goblin huckers + 30 goblins lurk outside. After bell rings, ogres advance to 120 ft and begin hurling goblins over the wall.
