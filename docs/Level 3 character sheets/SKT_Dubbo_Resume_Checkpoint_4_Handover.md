## Recent chronology since Bookmark 3

### 1. Keep contact and public oath

Alphie and Dorrin crossed the temporary bridge to Nandar Keep. Kella stayed at the gate with weapon down. Sydiri Haunlar, Alara Winterspell, Torem Breck, and Kaelen Zam were found alive with Lady Velrosa Nandar's body in the great hall.

Dorrin healed Kaelen's head wound with Cure Wounds. Torem's broken arm was confirmed ride-capable but not fight-capable. Sydiri and Alara were whole enough to fight.

Xolkin swore in front of Sydiri, Dorrin, and Lady Velrosa's body that until the villagers were rescued, his blades would serve rescue only: no claim, no recruitment, no pressure, no hostile move against Nightstone's people. Sydiri accepted the oath but warned she would pursue him lawfully if he broke it. Dorrin witnessed it before Berronar Truesilver.

### 3. Snake-alarm protocol

Alphie asked for a plain, non-faction alarm line. Kella trained the flying snake to recognise Torem. Torem holds the snake-token/alarm role at the keep.

Protocol:

- If a hostile force threatens Nightstone while the rescue party is north, Torem sends the snake north with a simple spoken message: force type, direction, approximate number if known.
- No code. No Zhentarim business. No report to the Snail.

### 4. Ear Seekers spotted

Before departure, Pell spotted movement from the south-west. Alphie rolled a **natural 20 Perception** and saw:

- Gurrash's orc warband approaching;
- Gurrash wounded but leading;
- Norgra One-Eye with him;
- twenty uninjured orcs behind;
- eight Ardeep wood elf scouts shadowing from the north-east treeline, watching and deciding.

Alphie chose to defend Nightstone first instead of riding to the caves with the orc warband at his back.

### 5. Nightstone defence orders

Alphie ordered:

- Western drawbridge raised.
- No one fights in the open.
- Archers/crossbows to towers and roofs.
- Wounded/support people and horses held under cover.
- The temporary keep bridge rigged to be pulled in if orcs found Area 11.
- No chasing. No opening the gate. Bleed them from the walls.

Pell and Maks operated the west drawbridge winches. Tessa, Sydiri, and Alara took tower/ranged positions. Kella took the trading post roof. Joren climbed the inn roof. Xolkin took central reserve near the south-west tower. Dorrin and Alphie held the ground reserve near the gate. Wren moved to support the south-west tower.

### 6. Horn invitation to Ardeep elves

With the orcs entering the moat, Kella urged Alphie to signal the elves. Alphie called for Sydiri to sound three short blasts to the north-east treeline. Sydiri did so. Rond answered with an Ardeep horn signal. The elves committed and began moving toward the orc rear.

### 7. Orc fight summary

The orcs entered the moat, expecting to reach the palisade. The palisade was sealed with tar and unclimbable by source. Wall fire and elf volleys tore them apart.

Key tactical moments:

- Dorrin cast **Bless** on Sydiri, Alara, and Tessa.
- Wren cast **Faerie Fire** on Gurrash, Norgra, and nearby orcs.
- Alphie's Portent **4** forced Gurrash to fail the Faerie Fire DEX save.
- Alphie used **Magic Missile** on Norgra.
- Alphie's Portent **5** forced Norgra to fail her concentration save, dropping Bless.
- Norgra later re-cast Bless and used Spiritual Weapon, wounding Sydiri.
- Kella killed Norgra with a hand crossbow + Sneak Attack shot.
- Dorrin healed Sydiri to full with Healing Word.
- Alphie killed Gurrash with Fire Bolt.
- Rond and the elves delivered rear volleys, pushing the orcs past the source morale break point.

Combat ended after roughly 3 rounds. Allied casualties: none dead. Sydiri was wounded and healed.

### 8. Rond persuaded

After the battle, Rond called, "Nightstone. You're welcome." Alphie thanked him without offering payment, named the captive children and villagers in the Dripping Caves, and asked for one more hour of help. Alphie rolled **Persuasion 20 + 3 = 23**.

Rond agreed:

- The debt of Gurrash was already paid.
- The Ardeep does not ride for human towns, but will ride for captives.
- All eight elves will help at the cave mouth.
- The elves know the chimney route.

## Important tactical lessons and current plan

### What worked at Nightstone

- Fortifications mattered more than raw HP.
- Do not fight orcs in the open if walls can do the work.
- Alphie's best leadership style is public, oath-bound, direct, and practical.
- Kella is most useful when given clear professional constraints, not moral lectures.
- Xolkin responds well to exact terms and public witness.
- Sydiri responds well to truth, respect for Lady Velrosa, and concrete military clarity.
- Rond responds to honour, captives, and shared enemy purpose - not payment.

### Proposed Dripping Caves structure

Rond's suggested two-prong plan should be the starting point:

**South mouth visible force** - draws goblin attention and pins Hark's fighters:

- Alphie
- Dorrin
- Wren
- Xolkin
- Joren
- Sydiri
- Alara
- possibly 4 elves as external bow support

**Chimney extraction force** - quiet entry near captives:

- Kella
- Rond + 3 elves
- possibly Dorrin if Josh decides healing/protection near captives is worth removing him from the south mouth

**Elf reserve/support**:

- Remaining elves with longbows outside the south mouth, cutting off fleeing goblins and preventing ogres from exiting freely.

### Marcus's tactical recommendation for next DM

The strongest non-metagame plan is probably:

1. Lower the western drawbridge, let the elves cross.
2. Re-raise the drawbridge after the rescue force departs or leave Pell/Maks ready to raise it immediately after departure.
3. Keep Nightstone defenders in place; do not clear every goblin building before the caves.
4. Ride north immediately.
5. Let Rond guide the force to the chimney and south mouth.
6. Avoid trying to exterminate the cave. Objective is prisoners out alive.
7. If Hark bargains, listen. If ogres threaten captives, neutralise or separate them.
